The Verdict

I can't fathom being told I wasn't allowed to make patients laugh and feel spiritually good if I were a physician. Yet that is precisely what Robin Williams' iconic character, Hunter "Patch" Adams, faces from a board of older, seasoned, gruff physicians who try to dictate his unconventional bedside manner. The overarching theme of sheer determination to do good shines throughout the message of this film. Williams does an excellent job cohesively winding the narrative arc like a pair of battery-operated chattering teeth—delivering a story that hits with both deep emotional resonance and genuine humor.

The Good

  • Robin Williams gives an extraordinarily warm and deeply empathetic performance.
  • Powerful overarching themes about compassion and humanizing healthcare.
  • Incredible balance of humor and emotional weight that stays with you.

The Bad

  • Rigid, cynical antagonists can feel a bit exaggerated at times.
