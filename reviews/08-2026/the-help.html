The Verdict

An absolute heart-rending masterpiece powered by a stellar, world-class ensemble cast. Octavia Spencer and Viola Davis share breathtaking on-screen chemistry, anchoring the film with profound emotional depth, humor, and unforgettable resilience. Their performances give a deeply resonant voice to the bravery of these women in 1960s Mississippi. Supported by exceptional performances across the board, The Help balances poignant historical drama with unforgettable moments of triumphant catharsis.
