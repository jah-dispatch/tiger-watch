The Verdict

An absolute sci-fi triumph that is unbelievably breathtaking from start to finish! Ryan Gosling delivers a brilliant, deeply charismatic lead performance as Ryland Grace, carrying the immense emotional and cerebral weight of a solo mission to save humanity. What I loved most about this film is how powerfully it propels the message that we must be willing to accept help and extend a hand to others in return. None of us can do it all alone—whether it’s surviving deep space, navigating life, or building love, it always requires a two-way street. With breathtaking visuals, humor, and incredible heart, this is an instant sci-fi classic.

The Good

  • Ryan Gosling delivers an incredible, deeply relatable lead performance.
  • Profound themes about connection, collaboration, and mutual trust.
  • Stunning visual effects and masterful direction from Lord & Miller.

The Bad

  • None. A pure 5-paw sci-fi masterpiece!
