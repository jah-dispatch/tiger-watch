The Verdict

The movie is reminiscent of a platonic love letter told between an unlikely friendship. Bryan Cranston delivers his excellence through the quadriplegic character he plays, balancing exquisite wit with raw vulnerability alongside Kevin Hart. When you rely on somebody else to handle the most basic tasks, you tend to want to get to know the person they truly are, and this film doesn't disappoint to teach us some of the most foundational life morals to date.

The Good

  • Incredible chemistry and balance between Bryan Cranston and Kevin Hart.
  • Delivers profound, heartwarming lessons on human connection and vulnerability.

The Bad

  • Can occasionally lean into familiar buddy-comedy tropes.
  • A few emotional beats feel somewhat formulaic.
