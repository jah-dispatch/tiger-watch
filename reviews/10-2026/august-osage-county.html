The Verdict

An absolute masterpiece and my personal #1 favorite film of all time. There is only one person who could have pulled off such an iconic, tour-de-force performance as Violet Weston, the razor-tongued matriarch: Meryl Streep. When her husband leaves for a fishing trip and never returns home, all tempers blaze as the family collides under one roof. Hiding deep secrets at every single turn, nobody catches a single breath for the entire runtime—and that relentless, high-voltage drama is exactly why this movie stands untouchable at the very top.

The Good

  • Meryl Streep delivers the single greatest lead performance in cinema history.
  • A flawless, powerhouse ensemble cast firing on all cylinders.
  • Razor-sharp, biting dialogue that keeps you hooked from start to finish.

The Bad

  • None. A pure 10/10 masterclass in family drama.
