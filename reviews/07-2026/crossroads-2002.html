The Verdict

I absolutely love every bit of this movie! Britney Spears delivers a genuinely endearing and sincere lead performance as Lucy, bringing a charming innocence and vulnerability to this classic 2000s coming-of-age road trip story. Joined by Zoë Saldana and Taryn Manning, the chemistry between the three childhood friends digging up their past promises feels authentic, emotional, and fun. While the pacing can be a bit irksome in moments as the plot shifts gears across the country, the warmth, iconic soundtrack, and sheer nostalgic joy make this a comfort film with super high rewatch value.

The Good

  • Britney Spears gives a surprisingly heartfelt and memorable lead performance.
  • Incredible screen chemistry between the core trio of friends.
  • A fantastic, nostalgia-packed soundtrack and fun road-trip vibes.

The Bad

  • Narrative pacing can feel uneven and irksome during certain transitions.
